## GLP-1 Clinical Trials (STEP Program)

### STEP 1 — Semaglutide 2.4mg in Adults Without Diabetes
- **Citation:** Kushner R, et al. Once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg reduces body weight in adults with overweight or obesity regardless of baseline characteristics (STEP 1). Indian J Endocr Metab. 2022;26(2):S35-S36.
- **DOI:** [10.4103/2230-8210.342310](https://doi.org/10.4103/2230-8210.342310)
- **PMC:** [PMC9067867](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9067867/)
- **Key Finding:** 14.9% body weight reduction with semaglutide vs 2.4% with placebo. 84.6% achieved ≥5% weight loss (NNT=2).

### STEP 2 — Semaglutide in Adults with T2DM
- **Citation:** Davies M, et al. Efficacy and safety of semaglutide 2.4 mg once-weekly in adults with overweight or obesity and type 2 diabetes (STEP 2). Indian J Endocr Metab. 2022;26(2):S36-S37.
- **DOI:** [10.4103/2230-8210.342311](https://doi.org/10.4103/2230-8210.342311)
- **PMC:** [PMC9067799](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9067799/)
- **Key Finding:** 9.6% weight loss in T2DM patients vs 3.4% with placebo.

### STEP 3 — Semaglutide with Intensive Behavioral Therapy
- **Citation:** Wadden TA, et al. Effect of subcutaneous semaglutide vs placebo as an adjunct to intensive behavioral therapy on body weight. JAMA. 2021;325(14):1403-1413.
- **DOI:** [10.1001/jama.2021.1831](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2021.1831)
- **PMC:** [PMC7905697](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7905697/)
- **Key Finding:** 16.0% weight loss with semaglutide + IBT vs 5.7% with placebo + IBT.

### STEP 4 — Weight Loss Maintenance
- **Citation:** Rubino D, et al. Effect of continued weekly subcutaneous semaglutide vs placebo on weight loss maintenance. JAMA. 2021;325(14):1414-1425.
- **DOI:** [10.1001/jama.2021.3224](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2021.3224)
- **PMC:** [PMC7988425](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7988425/)
- **Key Finding:** 17.4% total weight loss with continued semaglutide; +6.9% regain in those switched to placebo.

### STEP 5 — Two-Year Outcomes
- **Citation:** Garvey WT, et al. Two-year effects of semaglutide in adults with overweight or obesity. Nature Medicine. 2022;28(10):2083-2091.
- **DOI:** [10.1038/s41591-022-02026-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-022-02026-4)
- **PMC:** [PMC9556320](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9556320/)
- **Key Finding:** 15.2% weight loss sustained over 104 weeks (2 years).

### STEP 8 — Semaglutide vs Liraglutide Head-to-Head
- **Citation:** Rubino DM, et al. Effect of weekly subcutaneous semaglutide vs daily liraglutide on body weight. JAMA. 2022;327(2):138-150.
- **DOI:** [10.1001/jama.2021.23619](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2021.23619)
- **PMC:** [PMC8753508](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8753508/)
- **Key Finding:** Semaglutide 15.8% weight loss vs Liraglutide 6.4%. Semaglutide significantly superior.

## Cardiovascular Outcomes

### SELECT Trial — Cardiovascular Protection
- **Citation:** Lingvay I, et al. Semaglutide and cardiovascular outcomes by baseline HbA1c. Diabetes Care. 2024;47(8):1360-1369.
- **DOI:** [10.2337/dc24-0458](https://doi.org/10.2337/dc24-0458)
- **URL:** [Diabetes Care](https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/47/8/1360/156810/)
- **Key Finding:** 20% reduction in MACE, independent of HbA1c levels.

### Pooled SUSTAIN 6 + PIONEER 6 Analysis
- **Citation:** Semaglutide and cardiovascular outcomes by baseline HbA1c. European Heart Journal. 2024.
- **DOI:** [10.1093/eurheartj/ehae099](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehae099)
- **PMC:** [PMC11015952](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11015952/)
- **Key Finding:** MACE HR 0.76 (95% CI: 0.62-0.92); Non-fatal stroke HR 0.65 (95% CI: 0.43-0.97).

## Drug Comparison Studies

### Tirzepatide vs Semaglutide
- **Citation:** Semaglutide vs Tirzepatide for weight loss in adults with overweight or obesity. JAMA Internal Medicine. 2024.
- **DOI:** [10.1001/jamainternmed.2024.2525](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2024.2525)
- **URL:** [JAMA Internal Medicine](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2821080)
- **Key Finding:** Tirzepatide 76% more likely to achieve ≥5% weight loss (HR 1.76); 22.5% vs 15.8% body weight reduction.

### GLP-1 Meta-Analysis
- **Citation:** Comparative effectiveness of semaglutide versus liraglutide. Frontiers in Pharmacology. 2025.
- **DOI:** [10.3389/fphar.2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2025)
- **PMC:** [PMC12120964](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12120964/)
- **Key Finding:** Semaglutide superior to Liraglutide in HbA1c and weight reduction.

## India Obesity Data

### NFHS-5 Analysis
- **Citation:** Factors contributing to the change in overweight/obesity prevalence in India. Advances in Therapy. 2023;40:4794-4813.
- **PMC:** [PMC10611613](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10611613/)
- **Key Finding:** Overweight/obesity: Men 44.02%, Women 41.16% (NFHS-5). 16.7% relative increase in men from NFHS-4.

### India Obesity Prevalence (NMB-2017)
- **Citation:** Prevalence of obesity in India and its neurological implications. Annals of Neurosciences. 2021;28(1-2):39-45.
- **DOI:** [10.1177/09727531211003728](https://doi.org/10.1177/09727531211003728)
- **PMC:** [PMC8455012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8455012/)
- **Key Finding:** 40.3% overall obesity prevalence. South India highest (46.51%), East India lowest (32.96%).

### World Obesity Atlas 2026
- **Source:** [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/health-wellness/india-us-childhood-obesity-second-only-china-children-country-global-report-10564105/)
- **Key Finding:** India ranks #2 globally in childhood obesity (41M overweight, 14M obese in 2025).
